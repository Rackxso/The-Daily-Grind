About Us

The Daily Grind is where you come to focus, or to switch off. A quiet table and fast wifi when you need to get things done a good coffee and no rush when you don't.

We started this place because we were tired of choosing between coffee shops too loud to work in and offices too sterile to enjoy. So we built both into one room: a quiet side for deep work, and a slower corner for doing nothing at all.

Good food helps too. From fresh croissants in the morning to bagels later on, everything's made to keep you going whether that means one more hour of work or one more coffee with a friend.
